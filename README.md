# 🎓 Attendance Manager 

A modern, high-precision web application designed for students to track, analyze, and predict their academic attendance. Featuring a "Midnight Pro" dark aesthetic with neon accents and glassmorphism effects.

## ✨ Features

- **🎨 Premium Dark UI:** A professional "Midnight Pro" theme with deep slate backgrounds and glowing neon accents.
- **📊 Real-Time Analysis:** Instantly calculates attendance percentage with **2-decimal precision** (e.g., 87.27%).
- **🧠 Smart Suggestions:**
  - **Safe Zone:** Calculates exactly how many lectures you can bunk without dropping below your target.
  - **Recovery Mode:** Tells you how many lectures you *must* attend to reach your target.
- **🔮 Scenario Predictor:** Visualizes the impact of the next **20 lectures** (Bunking vs. Attending) on your final percentage.
- **💾 Auto-Save:** Automatically saves your data to the browser's LocalStorage, so you never lose your progress.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** (Semantic Structure)
- **CSS3** (Flexbox, Grid, CSS Variables, Animations)
- **JavaScript** (DOM Manipulation, LocalStorage Logic)
- **Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts)
